Textured risograph-print cut-out, like a screen-printed sticker cut from paper.
- Matte colour fields with soft, grainy gradients (orange to red, warm gold, mint to deep green, steel grey). No hard 3D lighting.
- Visible film grain over every surface and halftone or stipple dots in the shadows, as if printed in two or three inks.
- Shading is flat: one lighter tone and one darker tone per face, built from grain and dots, never smooth airbrush.
- Mostly no outlines. Thin dark ink lines only for small details: coin rims, the ₹ symbol, paper edges, bolts.
- Simple, slightly chunky shapes in a gentle three-quarter view, like the vault, folder and rupee notes in the references.
- Metals (gold, silver, steel) are flat grainy tones with a few paper-white highlight strokes, not shiny or reflective.
- Paper and documents are off-white with grain and thin grey ink lines; money is the green rupee note with ₹ and gold coins with ₹.
