# Worked example: "GIFT City Funds" (Varsity, Mutual Fund IP)

Source: the YouTube script (~1,840 words), the SB doc and 13 PSDs the design team delivered. Final cut ≈ 13 min, so the real pace is **~140 spoken words per minute** (with pauses, B-roll and screen captures). Timestamps below are real edit timecodes from the SB doc.

The SB doc colour-codes beats: **green timestamp = designed graphic (a PSD)**, **magenta = screen capture the editor records**. Lines with no SB entry stay on **footage** (anchor to camera).

Columns: **Time · Beat type · What the VO says · What went on screen · Why**

## Patterns to carry to the next video
1. **Section architecture is fixed:** the hero keyword ribbon, then the SOP of N questions, then a badger. Every section opens with an SOP repeat (highlight Qn). A sub-agenda inside a section is a 2-tile mini-SOP. Every "Difference 1/2…" or named sub-topic gets a ribbon header.
2. **Jargon gets a definition box** the first time it is explained (FoF, LRS, TCS). That is one box per term, never a box for a term the audience already knows.
3. **Synthesised comparisons become tables:** across funds, old vs new terms, option A vs B, rates. If the VO says "this table", there must be a table.
4. **Primary sources become screen captures, not graphics:** fund factsheets, product pages, RBI or regulator pages, NAV pages. The graphic team doesn't redraw them.
5. **Re-mentions reuse frames:** a later line about content already on screen is a "Repeat <id>, highlight X". No new PNG.
6. **Footage holds:** opening words, reactions, rhetorical questions, analogies, bridges, caveats without numbers, "how-to" asides and conclusions.
7. **Visual metaphors over literal text in the hook:** "invest in Apple…" became a vault with logo cards; "Indian MFs" became a folder with its parts.
8. **Density:** ~13 new PSDs in the first ~6 minutes, ~22 designed frames in total, plus ~8 screen captures over 13 min. That is roughly **one new graphic per 35–40 s**, or one beat (graphic, capture or repeat) every ~20 s.
