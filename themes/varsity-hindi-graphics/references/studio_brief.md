- **Footage / screen / repeat** beats stay in storyboard text only; every other graphic beat needs a full frame in `spec.frames[]`.

- **Assets:** `props/…` and `plates/…` paths from `assets/INDEX.md`. Green/red only on candle/bar bodies.
- Copy script numbers exactly. Match density of `scenes/example_spec.json`.
