# Assets

Upload reel-specific photos and moodboard cuts under `assets/` (e.g. `assets/props/`, `assets/refs/`). Reference them in frame `html` as `../assets/...` from templates or by filename in `plate` / inline `url(../assets/...)`.

Fonts are bundled under `assets/fonts/` (see `templates/fonts.css`).

Safe zone reference: `assets/safe_zone_guide_1080x1920.png`.
