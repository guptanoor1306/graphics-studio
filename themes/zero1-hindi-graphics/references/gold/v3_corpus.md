# Zero1 Hindi — "Kerala's Gold Obsession" (v3) — gold-standard storyboard corpus

**Episode shape.** A Kochi father, Arjun (the faceless wooden-block protagonist: red shirt, white kasavu mundu, fringed black hair, two dot eyes), buys gold for his newborn daughter, so the video asks whether jewellery is really the best "financial security". The **Intro** is set in his tiled-roof Kerala house: daughter's birth, a top-cam jewellery case, a time-lapse sandook that fills as she grows. The **SOP** puts a thinking Arjun with a gold chain among three numbered polaroid chapter cards. **Section 1** (Kerala's Tryst with Gold) runs on a recurring **vertical timeline spine on a graph-paper square** from 1 AD to 2000. Each stop has its own diorama: Malabar Coast pepper ships and Roman coins, 1950s paper-cutout families, Gulf migration, a "REPEALED" Gold Control Act stamp, pawn stalls, Manappuram/Muthoot/BANK gold-loan buildings and vault-to-cash collateral. It ends on "wear / preserve / pass on / borrow" cards. **Section 2** (Real Returns of Gold Jewellery) moves between Arjun holding the necklace, a recurring cardboard jewellery-counter set with a moustached jeweller, a necklace split into cost parts, a real WGC price chart in the sky and a **table-top "desk" device** (paper on a craft desk with ruler and scissors) for the sell-side calculation. **Section 3** (Same Gold, Different Returns) uses **rope-hung option boards in a cloud sky** (Option-1 coin/bar, Option-2 ETF, Option-3 MF), a SEBI-stamped "Gold ETF" paper, an NSE building, checklists, phone UI and desk-top tax tables. It closes on "The Returns Picture" (5 and 10 years later, back on the timeline spine) and a 30.6% gap staged above Arjun's family. The **Conclusion** jumps to the daughter's Kerala wedding (nadaswaram players, jasmine-rose garlands, an aged moustached Arjun). The Section-2 necklace comes back in a velvet box, then the bride in tears and greyscale "Memories" cards. The final shot tilts up to two hanging boards that split gold by purpose. Recurring devices: the timeline spine, the jewellery counter, the hanging sky boards, the desk-top table, the stamped document and the red-velvet jewellery tray.

But the polaroid thumbnails are recycled city-skyline photos (layer names "Screenshot 2026-06-24…") that have nothing to do with Kerala or gold.
- **Regional enrichment beyond the cue:** marigold-jasmine garlands and a pookalam for the birth (#2), a church + mosque Kerala skyline vs a Gulf oil-derrick town (#13), real Kerala gold-loan brands Manappuram and Muthoot beside a generic BANK (#18a), and nadaswaram players with jasmine-rose garlands at the wedding (#50).

## Top 10 storyboarding moves in this video

1. "2,000 saal peeche… 1950s… 1970s… 2000s" → one vertical **timeline spine on a graph-paper square** that stops at each year with a new diorama, then returns as "5 / 10 Years Later" → a single device carries roughly 12 historical beats and is reused for the future projection, so viewers always know where in time they are.
2. "Jaise jaise woh badi hogi, Arjun aur gold khareedta rahega" → the daughter grows in place through five labelled life stages while a carved sandook beside her fills with gold → a time-lapse BUILD turns a habit across decades into one accumulating image.
3. "Kerala se black pepper west jaata tha… Roman gold coins yahan aate the" → a pepper-laden ship sails "towards west →" and two Roman coins come back → a literal exchange makes the trade relationship readable at a glance.
4. "Gold Control Act ko hata diya gaya" → a real hand stamps "REPEALED" on an aged Act paper (the same hand and stamp later certify the Gold ETF with SEBI) → the source becomes a physical document and the stamp turns into a reusable "regulation" prop.
5. "Ab families iss gold ko collateral ki tarah rakhkar… loan" → empty vault → vault full of jewellery → stacks of ₹500 notes → a three-object flow explains a financial mechanism without a single number.
6. "₹1 lakh spend = ₹1 lakh Invest" → a faded thought bubble of the purchase with the equation written over it, then "But… actually that's not what happened" next to Arjun → BUBBLE for the belief, TYPO for the pivot: a classic set-up/knock-down.
7. "Making charges ₹9,900… Hallmarking fee ₹45" → the necklace itself is split into gold and grey parts with each cost pinned to a segment → the number is staged on the object, so the viewer *sees* how much of the jewellery isn't gold.
8. "Option 1 / 2 / 3" → a rope-hung sign in a cloud sky that swaps its text for each option and returns at the end as two purpose boards → a recurring DEVICE gives Section 3 its chapter rhythm and pays it off in the final tilt-up.
9. "Over 5 years… 10 saal mein…" → "The Returns Picture": a blue "Current value of Gold" card ringed by four instrument objects with their values, then the same layout updated → a BUILD on an identical layout makes the comparison instant and keeps the eye on the changed numbers.
10. "Iss necklace ka ROI kya hai? … ridiculous question … legacy aur memory" → the Section-2 necklace comes back in a velvet box at the wedding, the bride cries in extreme close-up, and greyscale "Memories" cards surround the aged Arjun → a CALLBACK prop plus GRADE for the past flips the episode from spreadsheet to emotion before the purpose split.
