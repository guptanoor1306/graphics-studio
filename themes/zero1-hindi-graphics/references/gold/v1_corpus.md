# Zero1 Hindi — "State Culture x Money Mindset" (v1) — gold storyboard corpus

**Episode shape.** One question, three states: three near-identical wooden-toy Arjuns (same age, ₹50 lakh salary, same info) differ only in the state they come from: Andhra Pradesh (veshti/white shirt), Kerala (red shirt/mundu) and Meghalaya (Khasi turban, jacket, red dhoti). The intro sets this up with the notebook-sticker name tag (NAME / AGE / SALARY / INFO / STATE "?"), turns it into a big fragmented question mark, then puts the three Arjuns on a 3D wooden India map. Each chapter opens with a map-zoom chapter card ("Chapter-N / <State> Money Mindset") and follows one money behaviour back to its history. **Ch-1 Andhra:** the kirana owner who won't touch MFIs, explained through the 1990s SHG → MFI boom → 2010 crisis, with a timeline spine on graph paper, one Andhra house that fills red / splits / gets notices, and govt/MFI buildings with signpost boards. **Ch-2 Kerala:** a bonus that goes to gold, explained through the Gulf-remittance gold boom (reused segment + YouTube UI), memory thought bubbles and a "Depression Babies" study. **Ch-3 Meghalaya:** Khasi matrilineal inheritance, with a living village set, a generational mother-to-daughter house, a family tree, dot/ball data animations and a piggy-bank sister. The conclusion calls back to the intro (three Arjuns, India map, influence collage). Recurring devices: the sticker tag, the 3D India map, the timeline spine with a red dot and a grid patch, the fragmented "?", sourced papers (newspaper, Stanford page, ScienceDirect board), dots for data, and a strict regional-authenticity note ("Is chapter ki sari chezein will be andhra style… Kerala chapter: kerala style… Meghalya chapter: Meghalya style").

## Designer deviations & additions

- **One recurring Andhra house replaces several separate graphics (#30–33, #39).** The tiled house on dry earth is pulled by LOAN-tag ropes, tinted 83% red with a hand-drawn arrow, then sliced into 4 vertical pieces covered in "OVERDUE / FINAL NOTICE / PAY NOW" notices. #39 brings it back as "Need Credit". The designer folded the 4-way split (#32) and the notices (#33) into one set, which works better than the brief.
- **Signpost boards instead of a notice board (#35–37).** The cue asked for one notice with 3 points. The designer planted three wooden signposts in front of a govt building (Ashoka emblem, AP-map reliefs, tricolour) and added the title "Andhra Govt. Had to step in on October 2010". #37 keeps the signposts and swaps the building for MFI. The result is a clean set swap (MATCH + BUILD).
- **Timeline built as a system (#16, #27–29, #55).** A left spine, a red dot, a huge year and a graph-paper grid patch. In #27 the designer drew an exponential MFI curve with a red "MFI" ball instead of the asked-for rising SHG line. #28 turns the number into a scene (SHGs building + crowd + hanging "1.7 crore Members" tag), and #29 inflates the timeline dot into a red disc to signal the crisis.
- **Added grade for meaning.** #23 is darkened, with spotlights on each SHG cluster, so the network reads as many points of light. #51new is a night scene with lit homes holding gold behind the 90% stat. #62 greys out India to isolate the NE. #64 desaturates the surroundings so the mother-to-daughter key handover pops. None of these grades were in the cues except #45/#80 (sepia).
- **Smart callbacks the cue didn't ask for.** #51.1 reuses the #6 shattered-"?" device, now filled with Kerala scenes, plus "Gold preference akhir ayi kahan se?", instead of the asked-for reuse of the #50 map. #49 puts the gold in a cardboard vault with a "First Choice" plaque. #24 uses a real hand pressing a "COLLATERAL FREE" stamp on the MFI building, mixing photo and toy.
- **Composition changes from close-up to wide.** #17 asked for an anonymous hands close-up. The final is a wide back-view at a bank cash-window, so "BANK ?" (#18) and the SHG sign swap (#21) can happen on the same building. #69 "outside Arjun's house" became an interior counting scene, and #56 (market + school + gold-shop family) was made as an intimate home scene of the mother trying on a gold necklace.
- **Missed asks.** #64 has no "MATRILINEAL SYSTEM" banner. #65 shows no key icons. #22 shows "Bank-1…Bank-4" instead of the accurate 90s bank names the cue insisted on (Canara Bank, Indian Bank, State Bank of Hydrabad exist only as layer names). #46 drops the "Keralite house". The #53 "20%" stat is not visible in the exported frame.
- **Wrong/reused assets.** #12's phone shows a leftover "NOIDA SOCIETY / Maintenance Fee / Payment Successfully" UI instead of a Take Loan button (the BANK/Take Now/Loan/Apply layers exist but are hidden), which breaks both the beat and regional authenticity. #51.2's YouTube mock-up keeps the placeholder title "When it rains, why Gurugram fails?" for the Kerala video.
- **Typos shipped to frame.** "SELF HELO GROUPS" (#21, copied from the cue), "SGHs" (#22–23), "Matrilinieal" (#63, copied from the cue), "Sikkam" (#61–62), "Our States Peoples" (Con#3).
- **Off-model characters.** The cash-window men in #17–21 (rounded heads, hair, sandals) and the #25 loan officer (human face, tie) are not the house box-head wooden-toy look used for Arjun and the Kerala/Meghalaya families. The Andhra Arjun is never actually seen at the SHG window, so the "Arjun paise nikal lega" beat (#20) is lost.
- **Correct fix of the cue's ref (#66).** The cue warned that its reference image had the sign reversed. The designer laid it out left-to-right as Men (6 blue dots) "<" Women (20 red dots), which reads correctly. The Meghalaya data frames (#65–67) also get a village/house footer strip so data slides stay in the chapter's world.

## Top 10 storyboarding moves in this video

1. Three people identical except for state → notebook name-sticker with SAME on every line and a red "?" on STATE (#3) → the whole thesis fits in one familiar object, and the "?" sets up the episode.
2. "Magar aisa hota nahi hai…" → the previous frame shatters into a giant fragmented "?" (#6), later reused for Kerala's "kahan se?" (#51.1) → the morph turns a pivot line into a visual signature that can be called back.
3. India map as the narrative engine → three Arjuns standing on their states (#8), then each chapter card is a zoom into that state with Arjun on it (#9, #46, #57) → one set carries navigation, chapter breaks and identity.
4. Arjun on the map → match cut to the same Arjun in the same spot inside his kirana (#9→#10), with shelves filling at low opacity for "imagine" (#11) → place and desire shown with no text.
5. "Magar banks se nahi!" → the bank sign changes to "BANK ?", then to "SELF HELP GROUPS" on the same building (#18→#21) → a single sign swap explains a whole system change.
6. Over-borrowing and recovery pressure → one Andhra house: LOAN ropes from all sides → 83% red fill → split into 4 → notices plastered on (#30–33) → BUILD on a single object makes stats feel like they're happening to a family.
7. Govt intervention and its fallout → the same dry-earth set with govt building + 3 rule signposts, then the building swaps to MFI with "Low Repayments / Low Fresh Lending" (#35–37) → cause and effect read as one set changing.
8. A 1990→2010 history → timeline spine with a red dot on graph paper, a year that runs forward, a curve rising, and the dot inflating into a red alarm disc (#16, #27–29) → time, growth and crisis in one consistent device.
9. "Ancestral property passes to the youngest daughter" → the same house across generations with a mother handing a key to her daughter while the world is greyed around them (#64) → inheritance becomes a physical handover that needs no explanation.
10. "Sole bread earner wife isn't seen as decision-maker" vs. patrilineal men → red balls merge and roll the blue ball out of frame, then blue dots encircle and shrink the red ball (#76–77) → abstract social dynamics become a physical push-and-surround that viewers feel.
