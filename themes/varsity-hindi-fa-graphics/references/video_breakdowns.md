## Video 2 (Module 3, Ep 5): Understanding the Balance Sheet (≈8:40, 9 PSDs, folder "Episode 5")

**Two kinds of beat alternate in this episode:**
1. **Concept/analogy beats get a designed scene (9 PSDs).** Assets, inventory, customers, vendor loan, liabilities, credit card, housing loan, equity, share capital.
2. **Annual-report beats get no PSD.** These are "show page 142 / 230 / 231, highlight as per VO", "associated note 5B / 16 / 19 / 14", "₹19,400 + ₹14,187 = ₹33,601 crores". They are built in the edit as **screen captures of the real Bajaj Auto annual report**, with highlight boxes or marker swipes on the line items the VO reads. About half the runtime is this.

So the rule is: **when the VO is reading a real document, show the document, not a graphic.** When the VO explains a term in the abstract or uses a life analogy, build a scene. Density: 9 PSDs in ~8:40, about one per 55 s. The report captures fill the gaps between them.

### What Video 2 adds to the rules
- **Document beats = screen capture.** Whenever the VO reads a page, note or number from the real annual report, show the report (page number from the SB) with highlights. **Never rebuild the report as a graphic**, and don't repeat its numbers in a separate graphic.
- **Scene-per-term.** Every balance-sheet term the VO explains with a real-world idea gets one scene: Assets → building; Inventory → factory line; Receivables → customers paying; Loans given → vendor; Liability → bank + borrower; Current liability → credit card; Non-current liability → house + long timeline; Equity → blurred title + chips; Share capital → company + investors + growing coins.
- **Money flow = gold ₹ coins on a dotted arc** with an arrowhead, from payer to payee (2.06, 2.15, 4.51). **Branching = blue elbow connectors to round icon badges** (white circle, 3D icon, label beside: 0.38, 5.38).
- **Big-word header.** In this episode the header is a **giant Inter Bold/ExtraBold black word (or two)** with the hero prop **overlapping the letters** (building over "Assets", bank over "Liabilities", house over "Housing Loan"). No coral bar. Ep 2 used the coral-bar italic header. **Both are house style.** Coral bar = section heading over a chart or comparison; giant word + prop = concept/analogy scene.
- **The designer keeps the analogy word over the term.** "Non-Current Liability" became **Housing Loan**, "Share Capital" became **XYZ Company**. When the SB gives both, the frame leads with the concrete thing; the term comes in the VO or the report capture.
- **The SB is trimmed to one idea per frame.** The personal-loan calendar, cash coming out of the card and "Personal Loan" callout were dropped or moved to hidden states.
- **Props are reused across episodes**: glass office building (0.38 → 2.06 → 2.15), bank (Ep 2 2.48 → 4.51), Bajaj plant photo (Ep 2 4.16 → 0.38), seated trio (Ep 2 → 7.28), magnifier, coins.
- **Characters get variants by role**: standing customers (three colours), standing vendor (speculator face, blue body), woman in a blue dress as the borrower, the seated trio as investors. Every scene with people uses **several distinct characters**, never clones.

### Errors to prevent (Video 2)
- `7.06(1)`: sub-head pills split as "Share Capital Reserve" / "Surplus". Correct: **Share Capital** / **Reserves & Surplus**.
- `0.38`: the "Liabilities" branch points to Loan / Electricity / **Machine**. A machine is an asset (plant & machinery). Keep each branch's items on the right side of the balance sheet.
- File named `7.06(1).psd`: naming should be just the timestamp.

### What Video 4 adds to the rules
- **Formula beats are their own frame**: every ratio gets (1) a header, (2) a definition **quote card**, (3) a **formula card** (the ratio name in a coloured highlight block, = , fraction), (4) a **calculation card** with the script's numbers, (5) an FSS scene/chart for the meaning. Each step is its own PNG or state.
- **OSA layout**: anchor fills the right ~45%; the card or text sits left of centre and must not cross the anchor's face. Cards are **frosted glass** (white 60–70%, thin white border, soft shadow, ~24 px radius).
- **Highlight block** behind a key term (marker style, the term's box filled): blue, yellow or green. Values to note get a yellow block. **Decided (Nov 2026): no green blocks.** Use yellow or blue; green/red stay on data marks and BUY/SELL.
- **Header variants**: plain ExtraBold; **coral underline under one word**; coral bar + two-line "Financial Ratios / Sub-category"; big glass title card.
- **Charts are glass cards**: bar (2–3 bars, per-metric colours as the SB says), grouped bars, pies with labels inside slices, three mini line cards side by side. The **mock Kite window** is reused for a real stock's price.
- **Scenes recycle the FA prop kit**: Bajaj showroom, glass building, bank, XYZ tower, seated trio, standing characters, coins, scale, magnifier, certificate.
- **Numbers**: the SB and the script disagree (EBITDA margin 21% vs 22.7%, PAT margin 16.5% vs 13.9%, Bajaj price 3,780 vs 10,379, P/E 20 vs 39). The finals mixed both. Skill rule: **use the script's numbers and state any disagreement in the storyboard notes**, unless the user says the SB numbers are newer.
